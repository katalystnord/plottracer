PlotTracer 2.0.2 - an error cap that jumped to the bar next to it

A single-fix patch release, and it undoes a regression introduced in 2.0.1.

Adjusting an error cap - dragging it along its own whisker to where the figure
actually draws it, which is exactly what the tool tells you to do with a
mirrored cap - could snap the cap sideways onto the neighbouring bar. The
recorded number went with it: the cap's Δ was then measured against the wrong
data point, so an export carried a real measurement filed under the wrong x.

It happened whenever a whisker was longer than the gap to the next point. That
is ordinary rather than exotic - a decaying curve with wide error at its
left-hand end and tight error at its right will do it on the first point or two
and leave every other point looking perfectly correct, which is what makes it
worth a release on its own.

The cause is worth stating plainly. 2.0.1 added the axis-lock that keeps a cap
on its bar, and to do that it had to answer a question: which data point does
this cap belong to? It answered by straight-line distance - and a cap 100
pixels below its own point can easily sit only 58 pixels from the next one. The
record answers the same question a different way, by matching on the axis a cap
does not move along, since that is the only axis that identifies it. Both
answers were already in the code. The drag used the wrong one, under a comment
asserting it used the right one.

The axis-lock itself was correct and stays. It now asks the record's question,
so the whisker you see and the number you export cannot disagree about which
point they belong to.

If you captured asymmetric error bars with 2.0.1, it is worth reopening them.
The failure is visible on the figure - a cap sitting on a bar it does not belong
to - and the Δ column will disagree with what you drew.

Nothing else changed, and nothing about the saved file format changed.
