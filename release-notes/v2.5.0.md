## See the figure on its own

Everything PlotTracer draws on a figure - data points, calibration reticles,
category ticks, grid dividers, error caps, measurement lines - can now be taken
off in one press. There is an **eye button in the top bar, beside the zoom
control**; press it and the figure is shown exactly as the paper printed it, with
none of our ink on top. Press it again and everything comes back precisely where
it was.

It is there for the moment you want to check what is actually underneath a mark:
a tick label a divider is sitting over, a hatch you are not sure of, a faint
gridline you suspect is a data point.

**While the marks are off, the figure is inert.** Nothing can be placed, grabbed
or selected, and the tools that would do those things are greyed rather than
merely ignoring you - each one says why on hover, and names the way back.
**Panning still works**, because looking around the figure is the entire purpose.
Your data is untouched throughout: the table on the right goes on showing every
point you have captured.

Hiding the marks belongs to the figure you hid them on, so opening another figure
always shows its marks.

## The category axis is measured, not declared

A bar chart's category axis is now read off the bars themselves when nothing
declares it, instead of relying on a count you type. Measured against published
figures, this took the assisted bar result from **36.7% to 45.5%**.
