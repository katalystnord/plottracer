## Automatic extraction joins the workflow

PlotTracer 2.7 is the first release of the automatic pathway. The workflow runs step
by step, **open → capture → calibrate → mark the legend → extract → correct →
export**, and each step is one card holding every way of doing it: a click, the guided
order, or an automatic detection. They all make the same marks, and any of them can
finish or correct what another started. On the figures it was measured on, it
calibrates the axes, reads the legend and extracts every series in a few presses;
every step stays yours to check, move or redo.

The rail follows the workflow, each tool with its number key: **0** Pan, **1** Edit
image, **2** Calibrate, **3** Legend, **4** Data extraction, then the editing and
measuring tools.

## Capture and calibrate

- **Capture figure comes first**, and the calibration card then stays on screen, so
  you can always see whether the figure is calibrated. Its heading carries the whole
  status and the button for the step you are on.
- **A graph type suggestion.** The captured figure is read offline and the type it
  looks like is marked **Suggested** in the picker (XY, Bar, Box plot, Pie, Map,
  Heatmap). Nothing changes until you pick.
- **The data area.** Drag a box over the plot and the automatic tools search only
  inside it, so labels, titles and axes are never read as data.
- **Calibration points go on labelled tick marks**, on every axis, a category axis
  included: Cat 1 and Cat n on the first and last category's tick marks, plus the count.
- **[Automatic axis detection]** places the points for you on XY, Contour, Histogram
  and the bar family, and the data area on the plot's frame. On a bar chart the
  category names are read from the labels and offered under each mark, with **Use
  them** and **Check them**; nothing is written until you use them. A drawn PDF figure
  is read from its own text; a picture by a chart-element detector and the OCR.
- **Any change to the calibration loses it**, and Calibrate sets it again.

## The legend

**Mark legend**, **Auto detect legend** or **Mask an area**. The legend and masked
areas are rectangles laid over the figure, no longer painted onto it, and every reader
leaves them out. Each legend entry's name and the colour beside it name the series.

## Data extraction

One card for every way to get points.

- **Auto-extract all series** reads every series the legend names with the tool that
  suits it, and reports one short line per series. One Undo takes it all back.
- **No legend needed.** Without one, a bar chart's series are its bar colours, and a
  line or scatter chart's are the inks in the data area.
- **Bars are found whole by the chart-element detector**, hatched ones included, and
  filed under the series whose colour fills them. Scored the CHART-Info way on real
  (PubMed Central) and synthetic (Adobe) test figures: 79.5% and 79.6% of bars found,
  and 95.2% and 92.4% of the boxes placed are bars.
- **Series in one ink are told apart** by their line style (solid, dashed, dash-dot,
  dotted) or by their marker (circle, square, triangle, open or filled).
- **Every problem is said once, in Places to check.**
- **Renamed tools:** Fill a region (was Segment fill), Follow guide points (was
  Interpolate), Split a line (was Cut).
- **Colour trace** searches the data area only, and takes touching markers apart into
  one point each.
- **The Eraser** erases everything inside a dragged box, on every graph type; a bar, a
  box or an error bar goes whole.

## Find lines

- **Dotted lines are read**: 96 to 100% of each dotted curve, where it was 0 to 51%.
- **Spectra are read through their peaks**: an IR spectrum comes back in 3 pieces
  instead of 23, a Raman spectrum in 9 instead of 64.
- A curve that simply ends where its data ends is no longer flagged.
- With several series, each is read as fast as alone: 1.3 s instead of 4.2 s on the
  four-series example.

## Also new

- **Figures drawn in a PDF** are read from the drawing itself, exactly, and the card
  says whether a figure is drawn, a picture, or both. Past 100% zoom it is redrawn
  sharp.
- **PaddleOCR replaces Tesseract** as the text reader, offline.
- **The Trace Challenge is traced by hand**: it tests your eye and your aim, so the
  automatic tools are greyed during a round.

## Changed

- **No Common origin on the bar family**, since Cat 1 is now a tick mark rather than
  the corner. Projects saved before 2.7 keep their categories.
- **Calibrate, Mark categories and Read cells pick no tool afterwards.**
- **The Categories stage has no # Series box**; nothing read it.

## Fixed

- Negative bars read 0 when Cat 1 sat on its tick mark below 0. A bar's value is now
  measured from 0 on the value axis.
- Colour trace placed a marker together with its same-coloured error bar, so the point
  read off-centre (by 67 of 820 h on the bundled example). It now sits on the marker.
- Closing a card by its rail icon opened another tool's card.
- Read labels could swap the words of a label: "Day 3" read as "3 Day".
- A drag was cancelled when it crossed a card's button.
- A colour pick armed on one figure stayed armed on the next.
- Corner grips swallowed calibration and Measure clicks.
- Turning the picture dropped the legend's kind; a graph type change dropped the data
  area, the legend and the masks.
- A refusal in the bottom bar outlived the next action that worked.
