## Automatic extraction joins the workflow

PlotTracer 2.7 brings automatic extraction into the workflow, step by step: **open →
capture → calibrate → mark the legend → extract → correct → export**. Each step is one
card, and each card holds every way of doing that step: a click, the guided order, or
an automatic detection. They all make the same marks, any of them can finish or
correct what another started, and nothing records which one was used.

The rail follows the workflow, each tool with its number key: **0** Pan, **1** Edit
image, **2** Calibrate, **3** Legend, **4** Data extraction; then Select, Error bars
and the Eraser; then Measure, Curve fit and Geometry. The ring in the lower right
corner turns while a long operation runs.

## Capture first, then calibrate

**Capture figure** comes first: frame the figure and press it. The calibration card
then opens and stays on screen, at least folded, so you can always see whether the
figure is calibrated.

- **A graph type suggestion.** The captured figure is read in the background, offline,
  and the type it looks like is outlined green in the graph type picker, with
  **Suggested** under its name. It can name XY, Bar, Box plot, Pie, Map and Heatmap,
  and says nothing when it is not sure. Nothing changes until you pick a type.
- **The data area comes first on the card.** Drag a box from one corner of the plot
  to the other: the automatic tools search only inside it, so labels, titles and
  axes are never read as data. It never stops a point placed by hand. With no data
  area, Find lines took the labels, title and axes for lines on every bundled
  figure tried; with the frame as the data area, nothing outside it was read.
- **Calibration points go on labelled tick marks**, with the number printed there,
  on every axis. A category axis too: **Cat 1** where the first category's tick
  mark meets the axis line, **Cat n** where the last one's does, and the count.
  Whether the figure draws its ticks at the categories' centres or between them is
  chosen afterwards, and the marks move on screen to show it.
- **[Automatic axis detection]** places the points for you on XY, Contour and the
  bar family: each on a labelled tick, with the number printed there, and the data
  area on the plot's frame. On a bar chart Cat 1 and Cat n go where the first and
  last category labels are centred, with the count, and once Calibrate is pressed
  the category names are read from those labels and offered, in the tick marks'
  colour under each mark, with **Use them** and **Check…** on the Categories
  stage's **Names** row; nothing is written until you use them. The stage lays its
  controls out in rows (Ticks at, Names, # Series), and a heatmap's Grid stage
  mirrors it. A drawn PDF figure is read from its own text and drawing; a picture by a
  chart-element detector and the OCR, offline. Every point stays yours to move or
  retype before **Calibrate**.
- **Any change to the calibration loses it**, a one-pixel nudge as much as an edited
  value or an option, and Calibrate sets it again. While a figure is not calibrated
  the table keeps its rows and greys out the values.

## The legend

**Legend** (rail 3): **Mark legend**, **Auto detect legend**, **Mask an area**. The
legend and a masked area are rectangles laid over the figure, shaded in two colours;
the picture itself is no longer painted over, every reader leaves them out, and
their corners can be moved. A marked legend is masked by itself, and what is read
from it (each entry's name and the colour beside it) names the series.

## Data extraction

**Data extraction** (rail 4) is one card for every way to get points: Place points,
Auto-extract and Cut are one tool now.

- **Auto-extract all series** reads every series the legend names, each with the
  tool that suits it, into a series of that name, and reports one short line per
  series. Find lines, run this way, is told the other series' inks, so another
  line's anti-aliased edge is not read as faint ink of this one. One Undo takes the
  whole read back; a second press replaces it.
- **Series in one ink are told apart by their line style.** Where the legend's
  samples share an ink and differ by their dashes (solid, dashed, dash-dot, dotted,
  or any other pattern, as matplotlib, R and ggplot2 draw them), the ink is read
  once, each line's pattern is measured and matched to the sample it looks like, and
  each series' pieces are joined into one line. On the bundled dash-coded release
  figure the four formulations come back as four series, each on its own curve.
- **Scatter series in one ink are told apart by their markers.** Where the legend's
  samples share an ink and differ by their marker (a filled circle, a square, a
  triangle, open or filled), each marker in the plot goes to the entry whose legend
  marker it looks like. Markers drawn over each other are taken apart into one point
  each. Markers that look nearly as much like another entry's marker are kept where
  they are most alike, and each entry with such markers has one place to check
  saying how many, so they can be moved with **Move selected to**.
- **Every problem is said once, in Places to check.** The card's report is one
  short line per series; a series that could not be read says so and points to the
  list, where the reason is.
- **Find individual series**: the Series row (rename in place, **+ Add**), then
  **Find lines**, **Colour trace**, **Fill a region** (was Segment fill) and
  **Follow guide points** (was Interpolate). **Trace by hand: Place points**. **Split
  a line** (was Cut).
- **Colour trace searches the data area only**; its own box is gone. On XY and
  Contour it reads markers, and Find lines reads the lines.
- **Colour trace takes markers drawn over each other apart.** Two or three markers
  that touch were one point; each is now a point of its own.
- **The Eraser erases what it touches.** A box dragged with it erases every point
  inside, in every series, on every graph type (it was Find lines' types only); a
  bar, a box or an error bar goes whole. A selection counts as one thing: it stays
  selected when the Eraser is picked, and a click on any of its points erases all of
  it in one Undo.
- One colour row for Find lines and Colour trace, with a small crop of the figure
  where the colour was picked, so a pick that landed on a hatch line shows it.

## Find lines

- **Dotted lines are read.** A dotted line used to come back as scattered label ink
  (0 to 51% of each dotted curve read, on figures made to test it); its dots are now
  chained into a line, the spaces between them bridged: 96 to 100%.
- **Spectra are read through their peaks.** A narrow peak whose two flanks fuse into
  one stroke used to split the curve there and lose the tip; now the curve runs out
  to the tip and back. An IR spectrum that came apart in 23 pieces is read in 3, and
  a Raman spectrum in 9 instead of 64.
- **A line that stops inside the plot is a place to check only when more of its
  colour lies ahead** of it, as on a dashed curve. A curve that simply ends where its
  data ends is no longer flagged.
- Told the other series' inks, it estimates the paper under the lines once rather
  than once per ink: on the four-series example a series is read in 1.3 s, as fast
  as alone, instead of 4.2 s.

## Figures drawn in a PDF

The calibration card says whether a captured PDF figure is **drawn**, a **picture**,
or partly each. A drawn figure is read from the drawing itself, exactly: Find lines,
markers (Scattered points) and bars read the drawing, the axes can be read from the
figure's own tick labels, and past 100% zoom the figure is redrawn sharp. Error bars
in a drawing are not read as data, and the reader says so.

## The text reader

**PaddleOCR replaces Tesseract**, offline, in its own thread: it reads tick labels,
legend entries and contour labels.

## Changed

- **No Common origin on the bar family.** Cat 1 is now a category's tick mark, which
  is never the corner P1 stands on, so the box that put Cat 1 on P1 is gone from
  Bar, Stacked bar, Line with categories, Box plot, Candlestick and Span chart. A
  project saved before 2.7 keeps its categories where they were.
- **Calibrate picks no tool afterwards**: the next step, the legend or the data, is
  yours.

## Fixed

- A bar's value is its one end away from 0 on the value axis. On a figure with
  negative bars, Cat 1 placed on its tick mark at the frame (below 0) made the frame
  the reference, and every negative bar read 0. Where the value axis has no 0 (a log
  axis, or an axis that starts above 0) the frame is the reference, as before.
- Colour trace put a marker's point at the centre of the marker and its error bar
  together when both were the same colour, so a point read too high or too low along
  the longer arm (by 67 of 820 h on the bundled asymmetric example). It is now
  placed on the marker itself.
- Closing a card by its rail icon went back to the tool used before it and opened
  that tool's card; closing a card now opens no other card.
- Read labels put the words of a label in the wrong order when one word's box
  started a pixel higher than the other's: "Day 3" was read as "3 Day".
- A drag on the figure was cancelled when its path crossed a card's button.
- A colour pick armed on one figure stayed armed on the next.
- Grips on a mask's, the legend's or the data area's corner no longer swallow a
  calibration or Measure click there.
- Turning the picture no longer drops the legend's kind, and a graph type change
  keeps the data area, the legend and the masks.
