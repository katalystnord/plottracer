## Phase A - curve separation (XY)

The gesture words below ("Follow curve", "press and drag") are first-pass
placeholders: a gesture cannot be right until David's hands have made it.

**A1 - DRAFT. Drawn order.** Given `cross-steep` calibrated, when the user
follows "loading" from its left end, the data table lists its points in the
order the line is drawn, from its left end to the right edge of the plot.

**A2 - DRAFT. A steep crossing is followed, never switched.** Given the same,
the table holds no point that lies on "unloading".

**A3 - DRAFT. A crossing is bridged by a fair interpolation.** Given
`cross-shallow` calibrated, when the user follows "slow" through its crossing
with "rising", the result is ONE series that continues on "slow" past the
crossing. Across the merged stretch, where the two lines share ink, the points
are interpolated between the confident points either side, and they look like
today's interpolated points (the existing `interpolated` role), so the user can
see where we bridged. ⚠️ That role belongs to the Interpolate tool today, which
drops and re-derives every `interpolated` point when re-run
(`calibrationSession.ts:3743-3776`); a bridged point must look the same without
being swept up by it.

**A4 - DRAFT. Unsure means stop, and say so.** Given `cross-tangent` (two
curves that touch and part), when the trace cannot tell which branch is its
own, it stops at the touch. The canvas shows a marker where it stopped, and the
prompt reads "Click the curve past the crossing to continue". No point past the
touch is recorded.

**A5 - DRAFT. One click continues.** Given A4's stop, when the user clicks on
"profile" past the touch, the same series continues from that click to the edge
of the plot, and the stretch between the stop and the click is interpolated
(as in A3).

**A6 - DRAFT. No bridge, no join.** Given A4's stop, when the user presses Done
instead of clicking, the series ends at the stop. Following the curve again
from past the touch makes a separate series.

**A7 - DRAFT. Dash gaps are bridged.** Given `cross-dash-solid` calibrated,
following "dashed" records one series across every dash gap, to the edge of
the plot.

**A8 - DRAFT. Never a silent switch, measured.** On the held-out figures, no
followed curve records a point that lies on the other curve without a stop
marker before it.

**A9 - DRAFT. The follow has a visible ending.** While a follow is in
progress, a Done button is on screen, mirroring Measure's area Finish. Done
keeps what was recorded.

**A10 - DRAFT. Discoverable from the screen.** The Auto-extract card shows a
"Follow curve" mode whose prompt says what to do: "Press on a curve and drag a
short way along it."

**A11 - DRAFT. Greyed, not hidden.** In the Auto-extract card, a mode the graph
type does not support is shown greyed, and its tooltip says which graph type
supports it (today such modes are hidden).
